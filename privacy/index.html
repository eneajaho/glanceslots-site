Privacy Policy

Glance Slots for iPhone and Apple Watch · Effective 2026-09-06

Short version: Glance Slots collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising. The App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”, and this page explains why that is true.

What the app stores

The slots you create (names, text, dates, settings) are stored on your device in a container shared between the app and its widgets, and on your paired Apple Watch. If iCloud Drive is enabled for the app, a copy is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple’s key-value storage so your slots survive a reinstall and appear on your other iPhones. Nobody but you and Apple’s iCloud infrastructure can read it; we have no access.

Permissions

  • Calendar — requested only when you create a “Next event” slot. Events are read on your device to display the next one; nothing is transmitted.
  • Contacts — requested only when you create a “Next birthday” slot. Birthdays are read on your iPhone to find the next one; only that name and date are stored, and relayed to your own Apple Watch.
  • Photos — a “Photo” slot uses the system photo picker, so the app never sees your library; the one photo you pick is stored, downscaled, on your device for the widget.
  • Battery level — read on your device and relayed only between your own iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch through your own iCloud account.
  • Live Activities — used only when you pin a countdown.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.

Shortcuts

Values written by your own Shortcuts are stored like any other slot content. Glance Slots never runs a Shortcut on its own.

Feed URLs

An “AI usage” slot can be given a web address you choose. Your device then fetches that address directly; nothing about it is sent to us, and the app never stores or sends any login token.

This website

Static pages, no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts.

Children

The app has no accounts or communication features and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date, and the app’s privacy label will be updated to match.
