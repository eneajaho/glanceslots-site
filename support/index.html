Support

Glance Slots for iPhone and Apple Watch

Email support@glanceslots.com. Replies within two business days. Include your iOS/watchOS versions and, if it helps, an export of your slots (More › Export slots) — it contains only what you typed.

How to add Glance Slots to your surfaces

Apple Watch complication

Touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to Complications, tap a position, scroll to Glance Slots and pick the slot. On watchOS 26 each complication can show a different slot.

Smart Stack

Turn the Digital Crown from the watch face, tap +, and add Glance Slots. Countdowns appear on their own during their final week.

iPhone Lock Screen

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the widget area, choose Glance Slots.

Home Screen and StandBy

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, search Glance Slots. In StandBy, touch and hold a widget stack to add one. Turn on “Black background” in the slot for StandBy.

Streaks, progress and one-tap logging

Add a “Streak” or “Progress” slot. Log a day or add one from the button on the Home Screen widget, the “+1” control in Control Center or on the Action button (pick the slot when you add the control), the Dynamic Island when the slot is pinned, or the “Log Streak Today” and “Add to Slot Value” Shortcuts actions. A streak stays alive if yesterday is logged, so you have all day to keep it.

Rotations, birthdays, and photos

“Rotation”: enter the labels of your cycle (Paper, General, Garden or Day, Day, Night, Night, Off, Off, Off), whether each lasts a day or a week, and the date the first label starts. “Next birthday”: allow Contacts access once; the slot shows the days until the next birthday and the name. “Photo”: pick one photo; it fills the Home Screen and StandBy widget, complications show the caption.

A number from a web page or API

Add a “Web value” slot with the HTTPS address. For JSON, enter a key path such as data.amount or items[0].price; for any other page, a regular expression whose first group is shown. Tap “Fetch now” to check. Widgets refresh at the interval you choose; the “Refresh Slot” Shortcuts action forces it.

Pin any slot to the Dynamic Island

Open the slot and tap “Pin to Dynamic Island”. It stays on the Lock Screen, in StandBy and in the Dynamic Island until you unpin it; countdowns end on their own. From Shortcuts, use “Pin Slot to Dynamic Island” and “Unpin Slot”.

Claude or Codex usage on your wrist

Add an “AI usage” slot (Provider: Claude or Codex). On your Mac, run bash scripts/install-ai-usage.sh --statusline from the Glance Slots project: it saves your usage windows to iCloud Drive every 10 minutes using only the files Claude Code and Codex write themselves. Then either build the “Update AI usage” Shortcut (Get File → Get Dictionary Value → Update AI Usage) or pass --gist and paste the printed feed URL into the slot so widgets refresh on their own. Full steps: docs/recipes/ai-usage-slot.md in the project.

Control Center / Action button “+1”

Open Control Center, tap +, add “Tally +1”, then long-press it to choose the slot. On Apple Watch Ultra, assign it to the Action button in Settings.

Frequently asked

My Watch battery disappeared from the Lock Screen Batteries widget after iOS 26

Apple’s Batteries widget now prefers AirPods when they are nearby. Edit the Lock Screen, tap the Batteries widget, turn off “Automatic”, and choose Apple Watch — or add a Glance Slots battery slot, which always shows both devices.

The battery slot shows a dash

Open the app on the other device once so it can report its level. Apple reports paired-device levels in 5% steps. AirPods have no public battery API, so they are not supported.

The countdown number looks wrong

Countdowns count whole calendar days and flip at midnight in your current time zone. Turn on “Include time” for hours and minutes near the date.

A widget is blank or stale

Open the app once; every save refreshes all widgets. If a widget stays blank after that, remove and re-add it.
